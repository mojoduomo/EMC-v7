Financial Identity

Two years of payment data becomes a credit history

A byproduct of metering. Not an add-on.

Because EdgeMeter devices simultaneously settle the financial obligation and record the consumption event, every payment automatically generates a structured credit record. Not through a third-party integration. As a structural byproduct of how the meter works.

Two years of consistent payment data — frequency, amount, regularity, time-of-day patterns — is a credit file. The raw material that every bank needs to extend a loan, every insurer needs to price a policy, every credit bureau needs to score a consumer.

The consequence, not the premise

EdgeMeter does not build a credit scoring model. EdgeMeter creates the data that makes credit scoring possible — for any institution, using any model, at any scale. The electricity meter has always known who pays on time. EdgeMeter is the first device that can prove it.
