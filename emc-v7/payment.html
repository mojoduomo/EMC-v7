Capabilities · Payment

Settlement happens at the meter.

Bank transfer, mobile money, card. Whatever rail the customer already uses. The meter knows when the payment clears and acts on it.

How it works

Direct integration with bank and mobile-money APIs. No middleware tier between the rail and the device.

Reconciliation at the meter. Settlement state is held on the device, not in a billing system catching up overnight.

STS-compliant for prepaid markets. Token injection automated at the edge.

Works with what's there. Mobile money first, bank rails second, card third — in whichever order the market needs.

Why it matters

Most prepaid systems break the payment chain into four hops: customer pays, vendor confirms, token issues, customer keys it in. Each hop is a failure point.

We collapse it to one. The customer pays. The meter turns on.
