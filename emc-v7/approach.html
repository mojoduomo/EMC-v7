How We Work

Dual-stack architecture.
Full spec compliance. Full intelligence.

DLMS for interoperability. MQTTS for everything above the spec floor.

AI Data Platform

JSON-over-MQTTS. Every meter event is a structured data asset — natively compatible with ML pipelines, time-series databases, and stream processors. No middleware.

Real-Time Device Control

Bidirectional MQTTS. Instant command execution — disconnect, reconnect, load-limit, firmware update. No polling. No waiting. The grid responds in real time.

Payment Terminal

Direct bank-to-device settlement. Supports existing mobile money and bank rails. Removes delivery failure points without displacing existing infrastructure.

Financial Identity

Every consistent payment builds a credit record as a byproduct of metering. Two years of payment data becomes a credit history.
