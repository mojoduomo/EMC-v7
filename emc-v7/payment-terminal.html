Payment Terminal

Direct bank-to-device settlement

The payment chain has too many points of failure. The meter can eliminate most of them.

In most prepaid electricity markets, the payment chain runs through multiple intermediaries before credit reaches the meter. Each handoff introduces latency, cost, and failure risk. When any link breaks — an SMS delay, a token entry error, a platform outage — the customer has paid but has no electricity.

EMC's payment architecture enables bank-affiliated settlement at the meter level. The meter functions as a payment terminal — settling the financial obligation at the point of consumption rather than routing it through a chain of intermediaries.

Supporting existing rails

This is not about replacing mobile money. EMC's direct settlement capability removes delivery failure points from the payment chain without displacing the existing rails that customers and operators rely on. A banking partner sits at the point of every electricity payment rather than downstream of a mobile money intermediary.
